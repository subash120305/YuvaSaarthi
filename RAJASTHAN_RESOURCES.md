## 🎓 Rajasthan Board (RBSE) Textbooks

### Official Website
**Primary Source:** https://rajeduboard.rajasthan.gov.in/books/index.htm

### How to Download RBSE Books:
1. Visit: https://rajeduboard.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Click "Books/Old Papers/Model Questions"
3. Select your class (8, 9, 10, 11, 12)
4. Choose subject (Mathematics, Science, Physics, etc.)
5. Download PDF

### Alternative Sources:
- **RBSEBooks.com:** https://rbsebooks.gkpad.com/
- **StudiesToday:** https://www.studiestoday.com/rbse-rajasthan-board-books-pdf-free-hindi-and-english-medium
- **AglaSem:** https://schools.aglasem.com/rbse-books/
