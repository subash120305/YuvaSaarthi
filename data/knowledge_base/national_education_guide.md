## 💰 NATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP SCHEMES 2025-26

### Top Priority Scholarships

#### 1. **NMMSS (National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme)**
- **Amount:** ₹12,000 per annum
- **Eligibility:** 
  - Parental income ≤ ₹3.5 lakh/year
  - 55% marks in Class VII (5% relaxation for SC/ST)
  - Government/Government-aided/Local body schools
- **Selection:** MAT + SAT test at Class VIII
- **Application Portal:** https://scholarships.gov.in
- **Last Date 2025-26:** 30th September 2025
- **For More:** https://dsel.education.gov.in/en/scheme/nmmss

#### 2. **INSPIRE Scholarship (Innovation in Science Pursuit)**
- **Amount:** ₹80,000/year (₹60,000 scholarship + ₹20,000 summer fees)
- **Eligibility:**
  - Top 1% scorers in Class 12
  - Any stream eligible
  - Science aptitude preferred
- **Application Portal:** https://inspire.gov.in
- **Expected Open:** November 2025
- **For More:** https://dst.gov.in/inspire-scholarships

#### 3. **Central Sector Scholarship (CSS)**
- **Amount:** ₹10,000-20,000/year for top 20% Class 12 students
- **Eligibility:** Excellent academic performance
- **Duration:** 3-5 years (depending on course)
- **Application:** https://scholarships.gov.in

#### 4. **Post-Matric Scholarships (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)**
- **SC Post-Matric:** ₹380-1,200/month + fees (₹2,000-5,000/year)
- **ST Post-Matric:** Same as SC
- **OBC Post-Matric:** ₹300-750/month + fees
- **EWS Scholarship:** For general category, income < ₹1 lakh/year
- **Application Portal:** https://scholarships.gov.in
- **For More:** Check your state government website

#### 5. **Minority Scholarships**
- **Pre-Matric Minority:** ₹1,000-3,000/year
- **Post-Matric Minority:** ₹300-1,200/month
- **Merit-cum-Means:** ₹20,000 course fee + ₹10,000 maintenance
- **Eligibility:** Religious minorities (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Zoroastrian, Jain)
- **Application Portal:** https://scholarships.gov.in
- **For More:** https://minorityaffairs.gov.in

1. **Visit:** https://scholarships.gov.in
2. **Register:** One-time registration (OTR)

### PwD (Persons with Disability) - Horizontal Reservation

**Horizontal Reservation:** 5% across all categories (SC, ST, OBC, General, EWS)

**Types of Disabilities Covered:**
- Visual impairment
- Hearing impairment
- Locomotor disability
- Autism spectrum disorder
- Specific learning disabilities
- Intellectual disability
- Multiple disabilities

**Extra Benefits:**
- 10-year age relaxation
- Exam time extension
- Scribe facility
- Low vision aids

**Certificate:** UDID (Unique Disability ID) from ALIMCO

---

### Important Reservation Rules

1. **"Creamy Layer" OBC:** Those with income > ₹8L/year NOT eligible for OBC reservation
2. **Domicile:** 85% state quota requires state domicile certificate
3. **Certificate Validity:** Most certificates valid 1 year only - renew annually
4. **Marks Relaxation:** SC/ST typically get 5% marks relaxation in qualifying exams
5. **Fee Concessions:** Available in most government colleges

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES FOR CHATBOT

1. **Always Provide Official Links:** Never rely on cached information; direct users to official portals for latest updates

2. **Verify Dates:** Academic calendar varies by board and state; confirm with official sources

3. **Category-specific Responses:** Different reservation and scholarship criteria by state - always verify

4. **Certificate Requirements:** Most certificates valid 1 year only; guide students on renewal

5. **Domicile Rules:** State quota requires domicile - clarify requirements

6. **Portal Registration:** Most scholarships require National Scholarship Portal registration (https://scholarships.gov.in)

7. **Escalation Triggers:**
   - Specific admission cutoff queries → Direct to JoSAA/MCC/CUET portals
   - State scholarship issues → Direct to state portals
   - College-specific queries → Direct to college website

8. **Frequently Wrong Assumptions:**
   - NOT all states have same reservation percentages
   - EWS NOT separate category - for general category only
   - Domicile certificate NOT same as residential proof
   - State quota seats ≠ All-India quota seats

9. **Document Checklist for Student:**
   - Aadhaar, Birth Certificate, Class 12 marksheet
   - Income Certificate, Caste/EWS Certificate (if applicable)
   - Domicile Certificate (for state quotas)
   - PwD Certificate (if applicable)
   - Medical fitness certificate (for some courses)

10. **Top Escalation Issues:**
    - "I have 75% in 12th, which college will I get?" → Direct to counselling calculator/portal
    - "Am I eligible for this scholarship?" → Check portal eligibility criteria
    - "When is counselling in my state?" → Check state education department
    - "How to prepare for JEE/NEET?" → Recommend SWAYAM/DIKSHA resources
