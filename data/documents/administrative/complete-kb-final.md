## 🎯 SECTION 3: RESERVATION POLICY (DETAILED)

### Rajasthan State Reservation System

**Total Reservation:** 49% in state educational institutions

#### Category-wise Breakdown

**1. SC (Scheduled Castes) - 16%**
- Reserved seats in all state colleges/universities
- Applies to: Engineering, Medical, General degrees, Law
- 5% relaxation in minimum qualifying marks
- Fee concessions/waivers available

**2. ST (Scheduled Tribes) - 12%**  
- Reserved seats in all state institutions
- Special provisions for Saharia tribe
- 5% marks relaxation
- Fee concessions available

**3. OBC (Other Backward Classes) - 21%**
- Non-Creamy Layer only
- Annual family income must be below ₹8 lakh
- OBC-NCL certificate valid for 1 year
- 3-5% marks relaxation

**4. MBC (More Backward Classes)**
- Sub-category within OBC quota
- Additional benefits in certain cases
- Included in 21% OBC reservation

**5. EWS (Economically Weaker Sections) - 10%**
- For General category students only
- **Income Criteria:** Annual family income below ₹8 lakh
- **Property Criteria:**
  - Agricultural land < 5 acres
  - Residential plot < 1000 sq ft in notified municipalities
  - Residential plot < 100 sq yards in other areas
- Certificate valid for 1 year
- Available if NOT eligible for SC/ST/OBC

**6. PwD (Persons with Disabilities) - 3-5%**
- Horizontal reservation (across all categories)
- Minimum 40% disability required
- Medical board certificate mandatory

**For more info:** https://gpck.rajasthan.gov.in/circularorders/Reservation%20in%20Govt%20Service%20in%20Rajasthan.pdf

### Required Certificates for Reservation

#### SC Certificate
- **Issued by:** Tehsildar/SDM/District Magistrate
- **Validity:** Permanent (unless change in status)
- **Required for:** Admission, scholarship, fee concession

#### ST Certificate
- **Issued by:** Tehsildar/SDM/District Magistrate
- **Validity:** Permanent
- **Special Note:** Saharia tribe gets additional benefits

#### OBC-NCL Certificate
- **Full Form:** Other Backward Classes - Non-Creamy Layer
- **Issued by:** Tehsildar/SDM
- **Validity:** 1 year from date of issue
- **Renewal:** Must renew annually
- **Income Limit:** Family income < ₹8 lakh per annum
- **Important:** "Creamy Layer" (high income) OBC students NOT eligible

#### EWS Certificate
- **Issued by:** Tehsildar/Revenue Officer
- **Validity:** 1 year (for academic session)
- **Income Criteria:** Family income < ₹8 lakh
- **Property Criteria:** Restrictions on land/residential property
- **Who Can Apply:** General category only (not SC/ST/OBC)

#### Disability Certificate
- **Issued by:** Medical Board (District/State level)
- **Minimum Disability:** 40% or more
- **Types:** Locomotor, Visual, Hearing impairment, etc.

**For more info:** https://dceapp.rajasthan.gov.in

### Scholarship Application Process (Common Steps)

**Step 1: Registration**
- Visit scholarship portal
- Register with mobile number and email
- Create password, note registration number

**Step 2: Login & Fill Form**
- Login with credentials
- Fill personal details (name, DOB, gender, category, etc.)
- Fill academic details (school/college, course, marks)
- Fill family details (parents' name, occupation, income)
- Fill bank details (student's own account - mandatory)

**Step 3: Upload Documents**
- **File Format:** PDF or JPG only
- **File Size:** Maximum 200-500 KB per document
- **Quality:** Clear, readable scanned copies
- **Common Documents:**
  - Aadhaar card
  - Bank passbook (first page with account details)
  - Income certificate (issued within 6 months-1 year)
  - Caste/category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Minority)
  - Previous year marksheet
  - Current year fee receipt/admission letter
  - Passport photo
  - Domicile certificate
  - Jan Aadhaar/Bhamashah (Rajasthan)

**Step 4: Preview & Submit**
- Preview entire application
- Check all details carefully
- Submit application
- **Note application/registration number**
- Take printout for records

**Step 5: Institute Verification**
- College/school verifies your application
- Verification must happen within 15-30 days
- Track status online

**Step 6: Disbursement**
- After approval, amount directly transferred to bank account
- May take 1-3 months after verification
- Check status regularly
