The Problem We Solve

1 counselor per 1,00,000 students

India's education crisis isn't just about content — it's about access, language, and personalised guidance that reaches every child, from Kochi to Kohima.

🗣️

Language Barrier

Over 70% of India's students learn in their mother tongue, yet most AI tools only support English. Knowledge locked behind a language is knowledge denied.

📡

Information Overload

Thousands of YouTube channels, PDFs, and coaching apps — but no single trusted voice that curates, contextualises, and delivers what a student actually needs today.

🧭

No Personal Guidance

Toppers have mentors. Everyone else improvises. YuvaSaarthi gives every student a Socratic guide that remembers where they struggle and pushes them to think deeper.

What It Can Do

22 features. One mission: your success

From offline RAG to real-time voice, from spaced repetition to distress interception — YuvaSaarthi is engineered to be a full-stack learning companion.

📚

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

ChromaDB-backed RAG retrieves verified NCERT, coaching, and past-paper content so every answer is grounded, not hallucinated.

🌐

23 Indian Languages

Full support for Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Punjabi, Gujarati, and 15 more — ask in any script, get answers in the same.

🎙️

Voice Input & Output

Students who struggle to type — or are on the go — can speak their doubt and hear the explanation back, in their own language.

📷

OCR for Handwritten Questions

Snap a photo of a handwritten problem from your notebook. YuvaSaarthi reads, parses, and solves it — no typing required.

🤔

Socratic Questioning Mode

Instead of just giving answers, the bot guides students to discover solutions themselves — building deeper, longer-lasting understanding.

🔁

Spaced Repetition (SM-2)

The proven SM-2 algorithm schedules review sessions at the exact moment before you forget — maximising retention, minimising study time.

💙

Distress Interception

When a student expresses burnout, anxiety, or hopelessness, YuvaSaarthi detects it and responds with empathy, resources, and human escalation paths.

▶️

YouTube Video Integration

Curated video recommendations from trusted Indian educators, surfaced exactly when the concept being discussed benefits from visual explanation.

🎓

Scholarship Finder

Integrated with MyScheme.gov.in — matches each student's profile to central and state government scholarships they are actually eligible for.

✈️

Telegram Bot Interface

Works on the phone every Indian student already has. No app install, no signup friction — just open Telegram and start learning.

📋

Teacher Lesson Planner

Teachers can generate structured lesson plans, quiz banks, and revision sheets tailored to the NCERT curriculum — in minutes.

👁️

Multimodal Understanding

Text, image, voice — YuvaSaarthi processes all modalities so students can interact naturally, however they learn best.

Under the Hood

Built with serious engineering

Every technology was chosen because Indian students deserve a system that is fast, reliable, and truly multilingual — not an English product with a translation layer bolted on.

🖥️ Frontend
  • Next.js 14 App Router, SSR, dynamic routes
  • Tailwind CSS Utility-first responsive styling
  • React 18 Component architecture, hooks
  • Vercel Edge deployment, CI/CD
⚙️ Backend & AI
  • FastAPI High-performance async Python API
  • LangChain Chains, memory, tool-use orchestration
  • ChromaDB Offline-capable vector store for RAG
  • Groq · Llama-3.3-70b Ultra-fast LPU inference, sub-second latency
  • Sentence Transformers Multilingual semantic embeddings
