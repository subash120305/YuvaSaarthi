**Question:** "What are the minimum eligibility criteria to get admission in polytechnic after 10th class?"

**YuvaSaarthi's EXACT Response:**

```
To get admission in a polytechnic college after 10th class, the minimum eligibility
criteria are as follows:

1. Age Limit: The candidate should have completed 15 years of age as on the date
   of admission.

2. Qualifying Examination: The candidate should have passed the 10th class (SSC or
   equivalent) examination from a recognized board.

3. Minimum Marks: The candidate should have secured a minimum of 35% to 40% marks
   in the 10th class examination (the exact percentage may vary depending on the
   state or institution).

4. Subjects: The candidate should have studied the following subjects in the 10th
   class:
   - Mathematics
   - Science (Physics, Chemistry, and Biology)
   - English

5. Reservation: Candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC, ST, OBC, etc.)
   may be eligible for relaxation in the minimum marks requirement, as per the
   rules of the state government or institution.

6. Domicile: Some states may require candidates to have a domicile certificate,
   which proves that they are a resident of the state.

It's essential to note that the eligibility criteria may vary depending on the
state, institution, or the specific polytechnic program you're applying to. It's
always a good idea to check the official website of the institution or the state's
admission authority for the most up-to-date and accurate information.

Additionally, some polytechnic colleges may conduct entrance exams, such as the
Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) or the Polytechnic Entrance Examination, to
select candidates for admission. In such cases, the candidate's performance in
the entrance exam will also be considered along with their eligibility criteria.
```

  expertise in software development.

- Industry connections: Consider institutions with strong industry connections,

Hello, I'm YuvaSaarthi. I'd be happy to guide you through the admission process
and help you with the required documents.

To apply for admission, you'll typically need to follow these steps:

1. Check the eligibility criteria: Ensure you meet the minimum requirements for
   the course or program you're interested in.

2. Choose your program: Select the course or program you want to apply for and
   review the admission process.

3. Fill out the application form: Most institutions have an online application
   form that you can fill out. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.

4. Upload required documents: You'll need to upload scanned copies of the required

   - Academic certificates and transcripts: Mark sheets, certificates, and
     transcripts from your previous educational institutions.
   - Identity proof: Aadhaar card, passport, or other government-issued ID.
   - Address proof: Proof of your permanent and current address, such as a utility
     bill or lease agreement.
   - Photograph: A recent passport-sized photo.
   - Entrance exam scorecard: If required, upload your scorecard for entrance exams
     like JEE, NEET, CAT, etc.

5. Pay the application fee: Pay the required application fee, which may vary
   depending on the institution.

6. Submit the application: Once you've completed the application form and uploaded
   all the required documents, submit it for review.

Some common documents required for admission are:

- 10th and 12th standard mark sheets and certificates
- Graduation mark sheets and certificates (if applicable)
- Post-graduation mark sheets and certificates (if applicable)
- Entrance exam scorecard (if required)
- Transfer certificate (if you're changing institutions)
- Migration certificate (if you're changing institutions)
- Character certificate (if required)
- Medical certificate (if...)
